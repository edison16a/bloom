## Recreating the references

**Photo as a neon LED wall** (finance page)
> Use dot-art. Turn /public/hall.jpg into a wall of small square LEDs on near-black. Contours glow brighter than surfaces, flat areas fall away to almost nothing. Colour runs left to right from teal through blue and violet to magenta, highlights go white. Slow drift and a light sweep, 8 second loop.

**Floating product icons** (solutions page)
> Use dot-art. Here are nine SVGs. Inflate each into a soft 3D pillow, float them at different depths and tilts on a dark LED wall, 4 px squares, each in its own two-colour ramp to white. Faint unlit grid behind, a few stray pixels around them, soft glow. They sway, never spin.

**Tilted ASCII field** (about page)
> Use dot-art. Hero background: a raymarched field of bevelled tiles seen at an angle and rolled 20 degrees, drawn in ASCII. Faces grey and sparse, top and right bevels become filled blocks with the letter knocked out and an iridescent sheen. Keep it dark around the headline on the left. Tiles rise and sink in a wave.

**Dithered ribbons** (connect page)
> Use dot-art. Full-bleed background of flowing ribbons, 4 px ordered dither, only #09090f #2a35d8 #5468ff #ffffff. Each ribbon has a hard bright leading edge, a long soft trail, and a hairline that shadows it. Leave 70% dark.

**Flower of letters** (video)
> Use dot-art. A pink flower made of letters, 10 second loop. A seed glyph types a short call, it glitches and collapses into a bud, the bud opens petal by petal while the camera turns, holds, then wind strips it right to left into loose letters until only the golden core is left, which becomes the seed. Dark wine shadows, hot pink body, blush highlights that spell the message. I want to read the petals, so keep the letters small.

**The same flower in hexagons**
> Same flower, honeycomb tiles instead of letters. Keep the depth.
