# Category archetypes

Pick the one closest to the company in step 1. Counts come from `research/logos.csv` (svgl categories and YC industries) and `research/case_studies.json`. They show where a category sits so you can choose to fit in or to stand apart on purpose.

## AI lab, model or agent company

Data: 107 AI logos in svgl. 44% are black led (47), then red 15%, blue 13%, orange 7%. Symbols are mostly abstract geometric (32) or a letterform of the initial (23); 14 are word only. Wordmarks are title case or lowercase about equally, set in geometric or neo grotesk sans.
Leaders: OpenAI (lowercase custom sans, knot symbol used sparingly), Anthropic (uppercase wordmark with a slash for a letter, warm terracotta), Mistral (pixel blocks, orange), Perplexity, Cohere, xAI (word only), Midjourney.
Default: black led wordmark in a neo grotesk at medium to bold weight, tight tracking, sentence or lowercase. One small geometric symbol. A warm accent (orange, terracotta, red) stands out in a field that is otherwise black and blue.

## Developer tools and infrastructure

Data: 36 devtool logos: black led (11), then blue, teal, yellow; letterform of the initial (10) and geometric (7) symbols. Case studies: Vercel (triangle, Geist), Linear, Supabase (green bolt), Raycast, GitHub, Replit, Cursor.
Default: lowercase or sentence case neo grotesk, tight. Symbol is one geometric shape or a cut initial. Black or near black with one bright accent (green, orange, electric blue).

## B2B software and SaaS

Data: 341 svgl Software logos (blue 26%, black 22%, red, green) and 860 successful YC B2B companies (blue 32%, black 14%). Letterform of the initial is the most common symbol (246 in YC B2B), then geometric.
Leaders: Stripe (lowercase bold geo, Blurple), Notion, Figma, Slack, Rippling, Attio, Ramp.
Default: lowercase bold geometric or neo grotesk, tight. Blue is the most crowded color; pick purple, green, orange or black led to stand out, or own a precise blue.
