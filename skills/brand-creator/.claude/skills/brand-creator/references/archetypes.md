# Category archetypes

Pick the one closest to the company in step 1. Counts come from `research/logos.csv` (svgl categories and YC industries) and `research/case_studies.json`. They show where a category sits so you can choose to fit in or to stand apart on purpose.

## AI lab, model or agent company

Data: 107 AI logos in svgl. 44% are black led (47), then red 15%, blue 13%, orange 7%. Symbols are mostly abstract geometric (32) or a letterform of the initial (23); 14 are word only. Wordmarks are title case or lowercase about equally, set in geometric or neo grotesk sans.
Leaders: OpenAI (lowercase custom sans, knot symbol used sparingly), Anthropic (uppercase wordmark with a slash for a letter, warm terracotta), Mistral (pixel blocks, orange), Perplexity, Cohere, xAI (word only), Midjourney.
Default: black led wordmark in a neo grotesk at medium to bold weight, tight tracking, sentence or lowercase. One small geometric symbol. A warm accent (orange, terracotta, red) stands out in a field that is otherwise black and blue.

## Developer tools and infrastructure

Data: 36 devtool logos: black led (11), then blue, teal, yellow; letterform of the initial (10) and geometric (7) symbols. Case studies: Vercel (triangle, Geist), Linear, Supabase (green bolt), Raycast, GitHub, Replit, Cursor.
Default: lowercase or sentence case neo grotesk, tight. Symbol is one geometric shape or a cut initial. Black or near black with one bright accent (green, orange, electric blue).

## B2B software and SaaS

Data: 341 svgl Software logos (blue 26%, black 22%, red, green) and 860 successful YC B2B companies (blue 32%, black 14%). Letterform of the initial is the most common symbol (246 in YC B2B), then geometric.
Leaders: Stripe (lowercase bold geo, Blurple), Notion, Figma, Slack, Rippling, Attio, Ramp.
Default: lowercase bold geometric or neo grotesk, tight. Blue is the most crowded color; pick purple, green, orange or black led to stand out, or own a precise blue.

## Fintech

Data: 223 successful YC fintech companies: blue 35%, green 14%, black 10%; initial letterform symbols lead (69), and 38 are word only.
Leaders: Stripe, Ramp (yellow, word led), Brex (orange), Coinbase (blue, lowercase), Mercury.
Default: lowercase or title case, bold, tight; a clean word led logo with a small symbol. Trust colors are blue and green; yellow, orange or black read as modern.

## Hardware, chips, space, defense, robotics, deep tech

Case studies: NVIDIA (uppercase, green #76B900, eye mark), SpaceX (uppercase word only, swoop X), Tesla (uppercase, T symbol), AMD, Arm, Intel, Anduril (uppercase Helvetica Now, black), Etched (uppercase wordmark). 6 of the 7 uppercase wordmarks among the 50 case studies are in this group.
Default: UPPERCASE wordmark, bold or black weight, normal to slightly wide tracking (wide is the one place it works), often word led with no symbol or one hard geometric symbol. Black led, or one strong saturated color.

## Consumer app, social, marketplace

Data: 217 successful YC consumer companies: blue, red, orange lead; initial letterforms (62) and pictorial symbols (48). 35 svgl social logos: blue led, initial letterforms (13).
Leaders: Airbnb (Bélo, Rausch red), Spotify (green), Discord (Blurple), Snapchat, Duolingo.
Default: rounded or geometric sans, lowercase, bold; a friendly simple symbol that works as an app icon first. Warm saturated color.

## Healthcare and bio

Data: 195 successful YC healthcare companies: blue 33%, teal 14%, orange 10%; initial letterform (47) and pictorial (40) symbols.
Default: calm, title case or lowercase humanist or geometric sans, medium weight; teal, green or a warm accent rather than hospital blue; avoid crosses, hearts and DNA clichés.

## Industrial, climate, energy, logistics

Data: 108 successful YC industrials companies: blue, black, green, red; initial letterforms and simple objects.
Default: uppercase or title case, bold neo grotesk or geometric; black led or one strong signal color; a hard edged geometric symbol.
