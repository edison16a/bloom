# Logo patterns

Research behind the brand-creator skill, generated by `scripts/summarize.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## Data

8332 logo files from 6259 unique brands. 8331 files were labeled by eye from contact sheets (see `labeling_guide.md`); every file was also measured from pixels (`scripts/analyze_logos.py`).

| source | files | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| simple-icons | 3472 | monochrome brand icons with each brand's official hex color |
| gilbarbara/logos | 2095 | full color tech logos, many with both a symbol file and a full logo file |
| svgl | 816 | modern tech and AI logos, with separate wordmark files |
| YC | 1703 | YC companies that reached Series A or later, went public, were acquired, or are YC top companies (raster thumbnails) |
| Wikidata | 246 | tech companies with a logo and 5 or more Wikipedia language editions (download stopped by Wikimedia rate limits) |

Labels are judgments by vision models, about plus or minus 5 points. The craft score is the labelers' rating of professional quality; it is how this research separates good from weak logos, so treat craft 4+ as the target and craft 1-2 as what to avoid. simple-icons glyphs are single color by construction, so their color counts are left out of color tables and only their official hex is used.
