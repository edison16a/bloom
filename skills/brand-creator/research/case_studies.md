# Logo case studies: 50 iconic tech brands

Researched November 2026 from brand guideline pages, press kits, agency case studies and design press. The structured data is in `case_studies.json`. Where a fact could not be found it is recorded as null. Values marked third party come from palette sites or community reconstructions, not from the company, and should be treated as approximate. Anthropic and Claude share one entry, as do OpenAI and ChatGPT.

## Patterns across these brands

All counts below come only from the 50 records in `case_studies.json`. Unknown values are left out of the denominators.

**Custom typefaces are the norm for leaders.** 26 brands use a custom, commissioned or modified typeface for the wordmark or the brand (for example OpenAI Sans, Product Sans, Netflix Sans, Airbnb Cereal, Uber Move, Coinbase Sans, Figma Sans, Geist, Mona Sans, Amazon Logo Sans, Cursor Gothic, the Cohere family). Only 5 brands are confirmed to rely on retail fonts (Anduril with Helvetica Now, Perplexity with FK Display and FK Grotesk, Snowflake with Texta and Lato, Rippling with Frere-Jones families, Replit with Diatype). For 19 brands the typeface could not be confirmed. Nearly every big rebrand since 2015 (Google, Netflix, Airbnb, Uber, Coinbase, Figma, OpenAI, Amazon, Cursor, Cohere, Vercel) shipped a typeface together with the logo.

**Lowercase and sentence case dominate.** Of the 31 wordmarks whose case is recorded, 11 are all lowercase (Amazon, Airbnb, Stripe, Slack, Perplexity, Databricks, Snowflake, Coinbase, Supabase, Intel, Arm), 9 are sentence case (Google, Microsoft, Meta, Spotify, Uber, Linear, Vercel, Figma, Notion), 4 use internal capitals (OpenAI, xAI, ElevenLabs, GitHub), and 7 are all uppercase. The uppercase group is mostly hardware, defense or deep tech: NVIDIA, SpaceX, Tesla, AMD, Anduril and Anthropic, plus Netflix.

**Most have a symbol, but the word usually leads.** 41 brands have a symbol, 5 are word only (SpaceX, Uber, xAI, Intel, Arm), and 4 are unknown. Several guidelines say the symbol may only stand alone where the brand is already established or space is tight (Linear, Vercel, Discord, GitHub, Spotify, Runway). OpenAI and Runway go further and forbid putting the symbol next to the wordmark.

**Black and white is the single largest color family.** Among the 41 brands with a known primary color family, 13 lead with black or monochrome (Apple, Uber, Linear, Vercel, Notion, Palantir, Anduril, AMD, Arm, Framer, Runway, ElevenLabs, GitHub). Next come orange with 6 (Anthropic, Amazon, Mistral, Databricks, Brex, Replit), blue with 5 (Meta, Snowflake, Coinbase, Intel, Dropbox) plus Discord's blue violet, multicolor with 5 (Google, Microsoft, Figma, Slack, Arc), red with 4 (Tesla, Netflix, Airbnb, Raycast), green with 3 (NVIDIA, Spotify, Supabase), purple with 2 (Stripe, Rippling) and yellow with 2 (Ramp, Hugging Face). Warm colors (orange, red and yellow together) account for 12 brands, more than blue and purple together (8). Among AI companies, warm color is a clear differentiator: Anthropic, Mistral, Replit and Hugging Face all avoid blue.

**Common hex families.** Orange reds cluster around #FF3621 to #FF9900 (Databricks #FF3621, Replit #FF3C00, Mistral #FA520F, Brex #FF5900, Amazon #FF9900) with Anthropic's softer terracotta #D97757 as the outlier. Reds sit at #E50914 (Netflix), #E82127 (Tesla), #FF5A5F and #FF385C (Airbnb) and #FF6363 (Raycast). Blues split into saturated electric blues (#0052FF Coinbase, #0061FF Dropbox, #0068B5 Intel, #0070F3 Vercel accent, #00A4EF Microsoft, #4285F4 Google, #29B5E8 Snowflake) and blue violets (#5865F2 Discord, #635BFF Stripe, #5E6AD2 Linear). Greens are bright and yellowish (#76B900 NVIDIA, #1ED760 Spotify, #3ECF8E Supabase). Dark neutrals are almost never pure black in the product: #232F3E (Amazon Squid Ink), #0A2540 (Stripe slate), #080225 (Arm), #141413 (Anthropic), #151515 (Raycast), #1C1C1C (Supabase), #101113 (Palantir).

**Named, owned colors.** Many brands give their main color a proper name and treat it as an asset: Blurple (Discord, Stripe), Rausch (Airbnb), Squid Ink and Amazon Orange, Snowflake Blue, NVIDIA Green, Brand Red (Raycast), Supabase green, Energy Blue (Intel).

**Designers.** A designer or team is recorded for 33 brands. About 18 came from an outside agency or studio (Pentagram twice, Collins, Wolff Olins, DesignStudio, Turner Duckworth and Koto, Gretel, Moniker, Geist, Basement twice, Smith & Diction, Sid Lee, Chimera, Sylvain Boyer, VMLY&R, DEPT, Regis McKenna for Apple). About 14 were made in house or by a founder (Google, Meta, OpenAI, Stripe, Figma, Linear, Notion, Palantir, Anduril, xAI, Ramp, Brex, Rippling, Replit). Startups tend to start in house and bring in a studio for the second version.

**Monochrome and spacing rules.** 24 records include some published rule on color versions, clear space or minimum size. Typical clear space rules tie the margin to a part of the logo: the height of the n (NVIDIA), half the logo height (Apple 2019), half the icon height (Spotify), the height of the symbol (Vercel), the x height (Snowflake), half the letter height (Arm), the leg height plus half again below (Runway), the logo height and three times the icon height (ElevenLabs). Published minimum sizes include 70 px for the full logo and 21 px for the icon (Spotify), 35 px or 8 mm (Apple), 24 px tall (Runway), 12 mm wide (Arm) and 0.5 in for the smile (Amazon). Almost every guide forbids recoloring, outlining, rotating, stretching and busy backgrounds.

**Meaning in one gesture.** The marks people remember carry one idea in one small detail: the swoop of the SpaceX X (rocket arc), the Amazon smile (a to z), the Tesla T (motor cross section), the Anthropic slash (code), the slanted dot of the Stripe i (now the whole symbol), the two bars of ElevenLabs (eleven and pause), the Airbnb Bélo (person, pin, heart, A), the Netflix curve (the cinema screen).

**Built from simple parts.** Several symbols are kits of basic shapes on a grid: Figma (three simple shapes), Slack (a speech bubble and a lozenge), Microsoft (four squares), Google (the G drawn from the logotype), Mistral (pixel blocks), Cohere (three cells). Cursor and Cohere go further and build the logo into the typeface itself.

## NVIDIA

The symbol is the eyemark, a stylized eye and spiral on a green field that dates back to 1993, paired since 2006 with an uppercase NVIDIA wordmark. Vertical lockup is preferred and the eye and word may never be separated. NVIDIA Green is #76B900 and the brand face is the custom NVIDIA Sans, though the wordmark font is not named. Clear space equals the height of the n. NVIDIA has never explained the eye, so any meaning is interpretation. It works because one saturated green and an abstract eye are recognizable on hardware without the name.

## SpaceX

A word only mark in custom uppercase lettering whose X has a long swooping stroke. Musk said the swoop represents the rocket's arc to orbit. No colors, typeface or designer are published; a credit to RO Studio is unconfirmed. The whole story lives inside one letterform.

## Anthropic and Claude

Anthropic uses a purely typographic uppercase wordmark, ANTHROP\C, where a slash stands in for a letter; Claude uses a terracotta spark. The studio Geist built the identity over about two and a half years up to the Claude launch in 2023, pairing Styrene (Commercial Type) for headlines with Tiempos (Klim) for text and a warm color system. Geist ties the slash to the code under AI and to the future ahead. The orange most often cited is #D97757, from third party sources. It stands out because it looks warm and literary in a field of cold blue AI marks.

## OpenAI and ChatGPT

The Blossom is an interlaced six sided knot pairing circles (human warmth) with right angles (precision). The February 2025 refresh, led in house by Veit Moeller and Shannon Jager with ABC Dinamo and Studio Dumbar, evened out its strokes and introduced OpenAI Sans, whose O is perfectly round. The wordmark now leads and the Blossom is used sparingly and never beside it. No hex values are published. The goal was one system and one identity that feels more organic and human.

## Etched

No official logo description, guideline, palette or designer could be found. The ETCHED word mark was filed in December 2023 and SOHU is a pending trademark. Every design field is null.

## Tesla

A stylized T that Musk describes as a cross section of an electric motor, plus a custom uppercase TESLA wordmark. Musk said he agonized over the lettering. A red of #E82127 appears only on third party sites, and the designer of the current mark is unconfirmed. A single letter that is also a piece of engineering lets the badge tell the product story.

## Apple

A bitten apple with a leaf and no wordmark, drawn by Rob Janoff at Regis McKenna in 1977 and monochrome since about 1998. The bite keeps it from reading as a cherry at small sizes. Partner guidelines allow only all black or all white versions, reserve gray for Apple, and set clear space at half the logo height with a minimum of 35 px or 8 mm. The brand typeface is SF Pro, made in house. It is a silhouette anyone can draw from memory.

## Google

The 2015 identity, made in house by Creative Lab and the Material Design team, set Google in the custom geometric Product Sans with four colors and added a standalone G and animated dots. The switch from serif to sans kept it legible on small screens, and the team boosted red, yellow and green because adjacent colors optically dim each other. Blue #4285F4 is widely cited; the other three hex values differ between third party sets. A plain word plus four primaries survives reduction to a single letter.

## Microsoft

Four squares in red #F25022, green #7FBA00, blue #00A4EF and yellow #FFB900 beside Microsoft in gray Segoe, introduced in August 2012 as the first change in 25 years. Designer credit is contested and no primary rationale was retrieved. The simplest possible symbol works from favicon to building sign.

## Meta

A continuous loop drawn as one line in 3D space that reads as an M from one side and infinity from another, in a blue gradient pulled from the core apps. The wordmark uses the company typeface made with Dalton Maag in 2019. It was built in house in 2021 to be viewed from many angles, which suits a company betting on headsets.

## Amazon

The smile arrow by Turner Duckworth (2000) runs from a to z under the lowercase wordmark. In 2025 Koto and the in house team widened the arrow, warmed the orange, drew a custom Amazon Logo Sans and built Amazon Ember Modern with the foundry NaN. The 2017 guidelines list Amazon Orange #FF9900 and Squid Ink #232F3E and set a minimum Smile size of 0.5 in. A double meaning in one stroke prints on every box.

## Netflix

Gretel flattened the arched red NETFLIX wordmark in 2014 and added the ribbon N for apps in 2016; Dalton Maag built Netflix Sans in 2018, echoing the wordmark's curve in its t. Netflix Red is commonly given as #E50914. One red on black and a cinema screen curve read as Netflix at thumbnail size.

## Spotify

A green circle with three sound wave bars beside the Spotify wordmark, which may never appear alone. Official rules allow green only on black or white, black, white, or monochrome elsewhere; clear space is half the icon height; minimums are 70 px for the full logo and 21 px for the icon. The green is usually given as #1ED760. Lineto lists a bespoke Circular for Spotify. A neon green reserved for the logo makes it the loudest tile on a home screen.

## Airbnb

DesignStudio's 2014 Bélo is one line that is a person with raised arms, a location pin, an upside down heart and the letter A, meant to be drawable by anyone. The color Rausch is named after the street where Airbnb started and is meant as passion without the aggression of pure red. The 2014 wordmark used a custom Circular; Airbnb Cereal with Dalton Maag followed in 2018. Hex values are community reconstructions (#FF5A5F legacy, #FF385C current UI).

## Uber

In 2018 Wolff Olins replaced the icon with a plain Uber wordmark in white on black, set in the custom Uber Move (with MCKL), inspired by transport signage such as Highway Gothic and DIN. The aim was legibility and a global mobility story. Retreating to a signage style word made the brand feel like infrastructure.

## Stripe

A custom lowercase stripe wordmark from 2016, made in house, whose i has a slanted square dot. In September 2025 that dot became the symbol: a white diagonal stroke in a blue square. The wordmark may appear only in slate, blurple or white. Blurple #635BFF and slate #0A2540 come from third party guides. The brand face is Söhne from Klim. One custom detail and a named color carry the brand.

## Linear

A sentence case wordmark, a logomark of a circle cut by diagonal lines, and a separate icon chip. Linear prefers the monochrome wordmark and reserves its desaturated blue mostly for backgrounds. #5E6AD2 comes from an unofficial database. Restraint fits a product that sells calm.

## Vercel

A solid upward triangle and the Vercel logotype, redrawn by Basement Studio alongside the open source Geist typeface (2023), inspired by Swiss design. Only black and white artwork exists, and clear space equals the height of the triangle. The simplest shape plus an owned typeface spread the brand through developers.

## Figma

The icon, designed in 2017 by Chris Hamamoto with Johan Prag and Rasmus Andersson, assembles three simple shapes on a modular grid into five colored pieces. It stands for design fundamentals, play like building blocks, and design as a system. A 2024 refresh added Figma Sans by Grilli Type. Secondary sources list #F24E1E, #A259FF, #1ABCFE, #0ACF83 and #FF7262. The logo is literally a component kit.

## Notion

A thick outlined block with a serif N, paired with the Notion wordmark; a 2025 update inserts the block into the word. The mark dates from the 2016 relaunch and is informally attributed to Ivan Zhao, with Roman Muradov's drawings defining the wider brand. Typeface claims conflict (Inter is reported for the brand). The hand made black and white look makes software feel like a notebook.

## Slack

Pentagram (Michael Bierut) and Slack's in house team rebuilt the hashtag in 2019 from a speech bubble and a lozenge on a grid, cut the palette from 11 colors to 4, and kept aubergine as an accent. The old mark needed a precise 18 degree rotation and broke on colored backgrounds. Hex values were not confirmed by any source found. It fixed reproduction while keeping a shape people knew.

## Discord

In 2021 Clyde left his speech bubble and the wordmark moved to custom lettering based on Ginto, with Blurple brightened to #5865F2 (plus Light Blurple #E0E3FF). Clyde alone is only for contexts where Discord is already clear, and assets are for digital use only. A character and a coined color give a community something to rally around.

## Palantir

An orb resting on a stand, after Tolkien's seeing stones, with custom sans lettering. Secondary sources credit Garry Tan and say the mark is essentially unchanged. A literary reference reduced to a dot and two strokes feels ominous and precise.

## Anduril

An angular black mark above ANDURIL in bold capitals, with Helvetica Now as the brand typeface and a near black, near white and gray palette. The in house design team owns the brand. Monochrome and angular reads as defense hardware.

## Scale AI

Sid Lee describes an identity built on a scale bar with a dynamic grid and bold digital colors. The page may describe a different organization of the same name, and no wordmark, typeface or year was confirmed.

## Perplexity

Smith & Diction set a lowercase perplexity wordmark in FK Display, chosen because its x echoes the symbol, with FK Grotesk for the brand. They wanted the feel of a Scandinavian subway system and a grotesk with character that works in many languages; lowercase mirrors how people type searches. Retail type chosen for one rhyming glyph feels custom.

## Mistral AI

A capital M made of pixel blocks, which the brand page calls an abstract pixel cat, in a yellow to orange gradient. The February 2025 refresh is credited by secondary sources to Sylvain Boyer Studio. The gradient is preferred, monochrome is for busy backgrounds, and recoloring or colored backgrounds are forbidden. Mistral Orange is given as #FA520F by a third party.

## Cursor

Chimera refined Ben Barry's cube into 2D, 2.5D and 3D versions in 2025 and drew Cursor Gothic on its Waldenburg typeface, embedding the logo and lockups in the font as ligatures to fix alignment. Horizontal lockup is preferred. Shipping the logo inside the font makes every lockup correct.

## xAI

A geometric xAI wordmark made of three connected strokes, made in house in 2023 and attributed by one source to Toby Pohlen. No typeface or colors are published, and the company was reported rebranded as SpaceXAI in 2026.

## Databricks

Stacked layered blocks in red orange beside a lowercase databricks wordmark, formalized in a 2020 brand evolution. Third party sources list Red Orange #FF3621, Gable Green #1B3139 and Spring Wood #F9F7F4. The stack pictures the data platform.

## Snowflake

A crystalline snowflake bug made of arrow legs that must always carry the registration mark, with a lowercase wordmark. The official palette is Snowflake Blue #29B5E8, Mid Blue #11567F and Midnight #000000 plus named secondaries, with Texta for headlines and Lato for body. Clear space equals the logotype x height. A literal name drawn as data arrows is easy to remember.

## Coinbase

Moniker reduced the mark to a blue circle with a square cutout (a coin and a hint of C) and built Coinbase Sans, 36 styles in five subfamilies. The blue was kept and brightened because it was one of the few recognizable old elements; #0052FF is cited from the press kit by a third party. The aim was digital finance with the trust of a bank.

## Ramp

The logo is described as custom drawn on TWK Lausanne, the brand typeface from Weltkern, with a solar yellow as the signature color. A roughly ten person in house team runs the brand. One loud yellow and one grotesk used with discipline made it a reference brand in fintech.

## Brex

The in house rebrand uses a flag symbol, a nod to the Moon landing and to customers planting their flag, because the old brand felt limited to startups. A third party lists the orange as #FF5900. Orange separates it from the blue of finance.

## Rippling

Rippling rebuilt its brand in house in about four months under creative director Nick Wiesner; Brand New's In-house In-Focus credits Frere-Jones Type families Magnet and Exchange. No logo description or palette was confirmed.

## Supabase

A green lightning bolt with supabase lettering. The official green is #3ECF8E with grays #1C1C1C and #F8F8F8; one color black and white versions exist and the files must never be repainted. A bolt and one bright green own the dark mode developer screen.

## Raycast

A logo mark and wordmark in dark and light versions. Brand Red is #FF6363 with preferred dark backgrounds #151515 and #202123. Dark versions go on dark backgrounds only. Coral red on near black reads as a premium Mac tool.

## Arc (The Browser Company)

Only a secondary description of a colorful gradient arc introduced in 2022 was found. Arc development stopped in 2025 and the company was acquired by Atlassian.

## Intel

In September 2020 Intel dropped the swirl for a flat lowercase intel wordmark with the e brought back in line, a nod to the 1968 original, set in the custom Intel One; VMLY&R is credited. Classic Blue #0068B5 is most cited (some give #0071C5) with Energy Blue #00C7FD as accent. Dropping the swirl made an old brand look current.

## AMD

An uppercase AMD wordmark followed by the AMD Arrow, two arrow shapes pointing up and right, in a proprietary typeface. One source says the arrow turned from green to black in 2013. No official colors were found. A forward pointing arrow gives a three letter acronym motion.

## Arm

A lowercase arm wordmark from August 2017, written Arm in text, allowed only in Arm corporate black #080225 or white. Clear space is half the letter height and the minimum width is 12 mm. DEPT says it created the visual identity. A humble lowercase word suits a brand that lives on billions of chips.

## Framer

An angular F icon and a Framer wordmark in black or white only; Framer Blue #0099FF and Deep Blue #0055FF exist but are not applied to the logo. Colored logos, outlines and using the icon as a letter F are forbidden. Color lives in the product, not the mark.

## Midjourney

A sailboat, read by commentators as a voyage into the unknown; no official explanation, designer, typeface or palette was found.

## Hugging Face

The hugging face emoji in yellow, with official colors #FFD21E, #FF9D00 and #6B7280, plus monochrome and pirate variants. The company was named after the emoji. Borrowing a symbol everyone already knows made an AI hub feel friendly.

## Cohere

Pentagram London built a symbol of three expanding cells that form a C, a custom Cohere type family whose headline cut has Voronoi cells, and a palette of natural and synthetic tones (coniferous green, mushroom grey, volcanic black, simulated coral, synthetic quartz, acrylic blue). The idea is new nature, mixing natural imperfection with computing efficiency. One generative motif ties symbol, type and color together.

## Runway

The wordmark is the primary logo and always stands alone; the symbol is only for small spaces and may never sit beside it. Black on light, white on dark; minimum 24 px tall digital or 1 in print; clear space equals the leg height plus half again for the descender. A strict word only rule feels like a film credit.

## ElevenLabs

The 11 icon is two plain vertical bars, abstracting eleven and echoing the pause button. Clear space equals the logo height, and three times the icon height for the icon. Basement kept the logotype in a 2025 rebrand that rebuilt color and motion for enterprise buyers. Two bars carry the name, the number and audio at once.

## Replit

Connected speech like shapes with rounded forms, used with a wordmark that may not stand alone, in Orange #FF3C00 with Diatype from Dinamo. The shapes suggest a move from code to natural conversation. Clear space is x on the sides and top and half x below. Older materials used #F26207, inspired by PLATO plasma displays.

## GitHub

The Invertocat (the Octocat silhouette in a circle) plus the GitHub wordmark is the default lockup; the Invertocat alone is for GitHub owned spaces. Logos appear only in black or white, with gray or green in a few cases, and never with effects. Mona Sans replaces Alliance as the brand typeface. A mascot compressed into a circle gives a playful brand a serious stamp.

## Dropbox

Collins and the in house Brand Studio flattened the open box glyph in 2017 and Sharp Type drew a custom logotype on Sharp Grotesk. The repositioning aimed beyond file storage toward creative work, and the logo color can change by context. #0061FF is cited by a third party. Keeping the glyph while letting color and type loosen up preserved recognition.
