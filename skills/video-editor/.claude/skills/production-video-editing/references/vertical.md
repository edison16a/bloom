# Vertical (1080x1920: Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

Same rules as horizontal. Only the layout changes.

- **Person:** stays on screen. Use one framing for the whole video: upper body, head in the upper half. Crop, never zoom.
- **Panel:** below the chin or above the head, 860 to 940 px wide, real content cropped to 4:3 or 1:1. Upper 12% and lower 20% stay empty for the app's own buttons. Same frosted frame, same fade, same one position.
- **Two things at once:** stack them, do not shrink them.
- **Name:** plain, left, around y 1300.
- **Captions:** always on, in the middle third above the panel or at about 62% height, 54 to 60 px medium white, 1 to 2 lines, soft shadow. No word highlight, colour or bounce.
- **Phrase:** 90 to 110 px, on the empty side or above the head.
- **First second:** speech or the strongest real picture, no logo, no title card.
- **Music:** opens in the first second.
- **End:** plain logo or nothing. Under 60 s unless asked.

From horizontal footage: recompose around the head using the matte, so the person is cropped and the room is extended from the blurred plate. Never letterbox with blurred copies of the same video.

Re-time every overlay for vertical, then run the same gate.
