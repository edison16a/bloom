There are **two different kinds of film**. Decide which one you are making first. They share text rules and restraint, and almost nothing else.

| | Type 1: person with overlays | Type 2: product film |
|---|---|---|
| Main picture | A person, on screen nearly all the time | The product, or images that build an argument |
| Product appears | Beside the person, in empty space, as a panel, phone, notification or text | Full frame, on a soft background, moving smoothly |
| Camera | Still. New framing only from a real second angle | Moves, but slowly and always settling on the thing that matters |
| Text | Name, a few spoken phrases, small captions | Short white or black lines, one idea each |

A hybrid is a Type 1 film with one Type 2 block inside, entered and left once.

## Type 1: person with overlays

**YouLearn (compilation, 1310 to 1350 s). The closest match to a founder on a webcam.**
Static camera, softly blurred room. Plain name with a small role, bottom left, up for about 5 s. When he names a thing, two real assets (scrolling slides at 13.0, a video player at 14.0) appear as rounded rectangles either side of his head, fade in over 0.3 s on the noun and leave together at 17. Only the content inside them moves. At 22 to 25 big white words sit left and right of his head, one per spoken word, some italic, partly covered by him. A short list builds on the left, one item per spoken item, then a three line phrase on the right, while he does not move. Logo full frame on white for 3 s. Then the product on a soft field, entered with one fast blur. Small captions throughout.

**Ghost, "We raised $11 million".**
Opens on dark macro shots of the device. Panels sit in empty wall or counter space beside the person and never cover the face: a line of text on the kitchen wall (37), a heart rate number and line with one small red chip (41), a document (43), pale chips joined by thin lines (45), two product photos (49), a three row list with thumbnails (61), a calendar on the right then message bubbles on the left (80 to 84), plain lines (85 to 88), cards (92), thin lines across a wide room (94 to 98), a panel over a laptop (100), a search field that becomes a picture result (115 to 117). Thin white outline, barely tinted fill, the room shows through. Parts arrive in order (outline, number, graph) in 0.2 to 0.4 s, then everything is completely still, and leaves in 0.2 s. New framing is always a real camera cut. No captions, lower thirds, emoji or bouncing icons. The logo outline draws on, fills, fades, then a tiny URL.

**Ghost, "An ecosystem will grow".**
Interview and hardware. Bold white name with a regular weight role below, bottom left, no box. Fades in 0.3 s after the shot starts and holds about 3.5 s. Two camera positions, cut at sentence ends every 2 to 6 s. B-roll is shot for the film, screens are filmed with a camera, a blueprint is drawn in thin lines (10 to 15). The logo rises over the last dark shot, then on black for 2.5 s.

**Vendo.**
Founders in a kitchen. A dot and a thin line point at the coffee machine: "40 *seconds* to brew". Frosted white cards (4 to 8.5) with the blurred room behind and a thin bright edge sit beside or just below people: the card fades and sharpens in 0.3 to 0.4 s, then its text and number fill in. Four or five spoken phrases appear as text in the whole film, word by word as said, medium white sans, exactly one word in an italic serif in the brand colour ("we are going to *race* them"). One product block (13 to 30 s), clean UI on a soft brand colour with small captions, then straight back to the people. A seconds count sits over the real machine because the story is a race. The last shot blurs through a coffee cup into white with the URL.

**CarSignal.**
Real garage and cars. 16 to 24 s is black, white words arriving one at a time and building lines. Frosted panels live in empty parts of the picture: inside a car window (25 to 27), on the garage wall, filling line by line (58 to 66). A phone call is two small waveforms on a blurred frame with a tiny step line (29 to 49). A phone in the hand (72 to 76), then its screen enlarged beside the seated man, scrolling (77 to 85), and again in a park (110 to 117). Notifications are pills with a real app icon, a bold title and one line, icon first, text a moment later. The URL is plain white over the last shot.

**Emergent (compilation, 673 to 761 s).**
Coffee macro shots, then founders at desks. Real LinkedIn, Slack and iMessage messages appear one by one either side of the founder (688 to 691). App windows float beside him in slight perspective with his head and laptop in front (748 to 753). A small pill panel at the top right (758). Short dark product blocks between people. Its glowing teal words are the part not to copy.

**Central Blue.**
Rocket, logo and tagline over Earth. Names small at both sides. A thin outlined circle labelled "Sun", arrows and a satellite line drawn around the head while the founder explains (8.5 to 12). "100K+ SATELLITES" rolls up once. Ideas shown on real mockups (billboard, magazine, phone, laptop, subway ad) from 51 to 62. A globe of lines resolves into the logo on black.

**Open Actuation.**
Dark macro shots of hardware. One white uppercase line on black (14.5 to 17), silence, then the name of the company large and white across the bottom of the founder's shot, one word at a time (18.5). Small captions from 20. Exploded CAD (37 to 47), real article captures joined by fast vertical smears (57 to 65), screens filmed with a camera. A flash to white, then the logo.

**Cascade.**
Fiction. Three short white lines in empty wall space, one after another, and they stay: "That's Gary. / His AI support agent auto-approved all refunds. / It cost the company $400k". The same device again at 27.5 and 39. Music plays inside the story and stops dead when the headphones come off. Product appears only as plain text rows or a logo beside the actor (53 to 58). Logo, one line and the URL on black, each alone.

**Atlog (compilation, 59 to 115 s).** Period spot, real actors, 4:3, one costume, one room per idea, no text at all until "atlog.ai" over the last shot. The product is on a monitor in the same warm room and the camera only drifts in.

**Donna (compilation, 964 to 1022 s).** Archive interview, word by word white text beside the speaker. The interview blurs behind one sentence. Black with one short line. A famous speaker clip with its caption, and two plain lines over it. Then a search field typing, one result at a time, tilted in slight perspective. The archive returns for the close.
