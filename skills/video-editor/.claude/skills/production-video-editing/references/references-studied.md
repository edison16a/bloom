There are **two different kinds of film**. Decide which one you are making first. They share text rules and restraint, and almost nothing else.

| | Type 1: person with overlays | Type 2: product film |
|---|---|---|
| Main picture | A person, on screen nearly all the time | The product, or images that build an argument |
| Product appears | Beside the person, in empty space, as a panel, phone, notification or text | Full frame, on a soft background, moving smoothly |
| Camera | Still. New framing only from a real second angle | Moves, but slowly and always settling on the thing that matters |
| Text | Name, a few spoken phrases, small captions | Short white or black lines, one idea each |

A hybrid is a Type 1 film with one Type 2 block inside, entered and left once.
