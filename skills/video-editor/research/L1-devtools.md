# L1-devtools: launch films from premium dev-tool and productivity SaaS companies

25 official brand-channel launch films (2023–2026). All were measured with `tools/measure.py` (the version that retries scdet at threshold 5 when cuts/min < 6). The overview sheet was read for every video, and the transitions and open sheets for most.

## Measurement caveats (read first)
- **The cut counter is the weakest metric in this category.** Many of these films have no hard cuts at all. Scenes change through continuous camera moves, morphs (a headline turns into an input bar, which turns into an app window), cross-dissolves on near-black, and blur-ins. Even at threshold 5, scdet found 0–2 cuts in 01, 02, 03, 05, 12, 24 and 25.
- So the table has two columns: **cuts/min (scdet)** and **scene changes/min (visual)**. The visual column is my count of distinct compositions or title cards on the overview sheet. It is an estimate (±20%), filled in only where scdet clearly undercounts (marked `v`). Elsewhere it equals the scdet value.
- **Some BPM values are probably half or double-time errors.** For example, 03 reads 72 BPM, but its transients repeat every ~0.28 s, which suggests ~107 BPM 8ths. 24 reads 76 BPM with hits every ~0.39 s (~153/77). I flag these where the hit spacing disagrees with the BPM.
- `cuts_on_beat_share` and `cuts_with_hit_share` are meaningless when there are ~0 cuts. Those cells are null or 0 for that reason, not because the cuts miss the beat.
- Nobody listened to anything. Every statement about SFX or music is inferred from waveform numbers plus what the frames show.

## 2. Per-video notes

**01 Linear Agent.** *Hook:* the dark app sidebar is already in a slow 3D-tilted push, and a +12.3 dB swell lands at 3–4 s. *Structure:* 0–6 s app shell; 6–14 s an "Ask Linear" prompt types in; 14–26 s results stream into a list; 26–40 s PRD doc and architecture diagram; 40–46 s full app (the only two hard cuts); 46–55 s end card "Linear. At your command." → logo. *Text:* the UI's own text types at roughly typing speed. The end card is a plain sans that blurs in, with the second phrase fading in about 0.5 s after "Linear." *SFX:* very sparse (13 hits/min), small UI ticks at 7–12 s and 18–21 s. *Music:* very quiet bed (-29 LUFS), one swell near the start and one at 41 s, under the hard cut. *Transitions:* the camera simply travels from one panel to the next, with depth-of-field falloff hiding the edges. *Flow:* a constant forward/upward push (Z+ ↑). The camera never reverses.

**02 Linear Releases.** *Hook:* white hair-thin branch lines growing on black, with issue IDs floating in. *Structure:* 0–8 s graph flythrough; 9–11 s "PLAN AND TRACK RELEASES" mono card (fades in, fades out); 11–15 s three tilted release panels fan in; 15–22 s graph again from a new angle; 22–25 s "RELEASES" typed letter by letter with a block cursor; 25–27 s "AVAILABLE NOW" typed; 27–30 s logo. *Text:* mono caps, small and centred, typed one character per ~2 frames with a solid cursor block. *Music:* calm 110 BPM pulse, music fades out over the last 4 s. *SFX:* nearly none. *Flow:* lateral drift (←), then a slight upward tilt. Atmospheric, not energetic. It's useful only as a calm-teaser template.

## 3. Batch synthesis

### Typical ranges (n = 25)
- **Length:** median 55 s (IQR 37–75 s). Most single-feature launches run 30–60 s; keynote-style or founder films run 90–150 s.
- **Cuts/min (scdet):** median 9.7 (IQR 2.2–15.9). **Scene changes/min (visual-corrected):** median 14.4 (IQR 12–24). The extremes: Figma Config 2025 at 68, Arc on Windows at 54, and the Raycast and Arc montages at 31–33.
- **Median shot (or scene) length:** 3.7 s (IQR 2.0–9.8 s) for hard cuts. Inside those "shots", the UI changes state every 1–2 s, so the *visual event rate* is far higher than the cut rate.
- **BPM:** median 103 (IQR 80–118); several readings are probably half-time, so the real tempo cluster is about 100–130. Beat strength median 4.0 (IQR 2.7–6.0), so most films have a clear beat.
- **LUFS:** median -16.4 (IQR -18.7 to -13.2). The energetic ones (Arc Windows -9.8, Loom -10.3, Vercel Ship -11.1, Arc 1.0 -11.5, Superhuman -11.6, Config 2026 -11.9) sit around -10 to -12. Calm and VO films sit at -17 to -29.
- **Hits/min:** median 54 (IQR 44–84). The busiest are Cursor 2.0 (112), Retool (102), v0 (101), Notion AI (100) and Config 2025 (99). The outliers are Framer 3.0 (0.8) and the Linear dark films (10–13).
- **Speech share:** median 0.03. Most are music-only; VO or founder pieces (Warp, Cursor 3, Zed, Arc 1.0) run 0.3–0.5.

### Pacing across the video
- Mean cuts/min by quarter: 17.3 → 18.9 → 18.3 → 12.1. **The pace is front-loaded or flat, and the last quarter slows** because of the end card, URL and logo (typically 4–8 s of calm).
- Three shapes recur:
  1. **Flat-out** (Config 2025, Arc Windows, Raycast Windows): 45–70 cuts/min from second 2 to the end card.
  2. **Quiet hook, then drop** (Linear for Agents 9 s, Vercel Ship 11 s, Notion 3.0 10 s, Config 2026 12 s, Retool 6 s, Arc Windows 15.7 s): 5–12 s of sparse sound and slow motion, then a +5 to +12 dB lift exactly on the first product action or title.
  3. **Accelerate → silence → reveal** (Raycast Focus): cut rate climbs from 19 to 47 to 61/min, then ~4 s of near-silence (-36 dB) before the product appears.
- Long VO films (Warp 2.0, Zed) build in the last quarter: Warp goes from 5 to 38 cuts/min with a 0.2–0.3 s social-proof burst; Zed rises from 5.4 to 12.7.

### How cuts relate to the beat
- Median cuts on the beat is only 22%; median cuts with a hit is 27%. **These editors do not cut every shot on the beat.** The beat lives in the music and in rhythmic UI SFX; picture changes are free-timed.
- What *does* lock to the grid: (a) the drop or lift moment, which coincides with the first big visual change in every "quiet hook" film; (b) SFX ticks, which run on exact 8th notes (Linear for Agents 0.28 s, v0 0.30 s, Retool 0.375 s, Loom 0.39 s, Config 0.46 s, Cursor 2.0 0.53 s quarters); (c) stat or title swaps in short bursts (Config 2026, Notion Mail at 59% on the beat).
- Inference: the "energy" comes from a constant 8th-note transient layer plus a loud, flat (LRA 2–7) music bed, not from cutting faster.

### SFX density and placement rules
- 44–84 transients per minute is the norm, about one per beat or per 8th. Most map to UI events: clicks, key-taps while text types, selection pops, cards landing.
- Typing is always sonified (clusters of ticks at roughly 10–15 per second in Raycast and Warp).
- Near-silent films (Linear Releases, Linear Agent, Framer 3.0) feel premium but low-energy. That is the user's complaint, so avoid this profile for an "energetic" brief.
- One big impact or swell per film, at the reveal: Raycast Focus +19 dB at 43 s, Raycast Windows +38 dB from silence, Vercel Ship +12 dB at 11 s.
- End cards: music fades out over 3–5 s (energy drops 20–40 dB in the last 4 s of almost every film); no SFX on the logo except a soft final hit in some.

### Text animation conventions
- **Font:** clean grotesk sans (Inter-like) in 20 of 25. Exceptions: mono caps for "technical" teasers (Linear Releases, Raycast New, Vercel dates), serif for heritage or story tone (Arc 1.0, Raycast "Now on Windows"), custom display type for events (Config).
- **Placement:** centred, 1–2 lines, short (2–7 words). Text cards alternate with UI shots (Notion, Cursor, Retool, Arc 1.0). Caption over UI sits top-centre (Loom, Superhuman).
- **Entries, from most to least common:**
  1. Per-word blur-in with a slight rise (Retool, Linear, v0): each word ~0.15–0.2 s, staggered ~0.1–0.15 s, from grey/blurred to sharp white.
  2. Typewriter with a visible caret (Cursor 2.0, Linear, Raycast, Notion 3.0): ~3 characters per frame-tenth at 10 fps, so roughly 25–30 characters per second for display text; mono typing in Linear is slower, ~1 character per 2 frames.
  3. Mask rise from below (Notion "Meet the new Notion AI", ~0.4 s).
  4. **Inline chip swap**: one slot in the sentence cycles every ~0.5 s with a vertical blur-roll (Notion AI, Warp "full terminal use").
  5. Glyph scramble or resolve for logos (Retool "Reto01", Config glyph cycling, Vercel "Ship" stencil).
- **Exits:** fade or blur-out over ~0.2–0.3 s, or a hard cut on the next card; rarely animated out.
- **Holds:** title cards 0.6–1.5 s in fast films, 1.5–3 s in calm ones.

### Flow and direction rules
- **Forward and upward dominate:** flow strings are full of ↑ and Z+ (new panels rise from below; the camera pushes into the UI). It reads as "progress / deeper into the product".
- **Leftward lateral motion** (content moving ←, i.e. the camera travelling right) is the standard for sequences and carousels (Zed, Framer, Notion caret-follow, Retool). Reading direction carries the eye.
- One-take films keep a single direction for long stretches (Linear Agent: Z+/↑ for ~40 s; v0: Z+ then →) and never reverse; reversals happen only at chapter breaks.
- Montage films (Raycast Focus, Raycast Windows, Arc) alternate ← → deliberately. Continuity there comes from a constant frame graphic (Arc's rounded border plus captions, Raycast's centred boxed archive frames), not from direction.
- **Match devices instead of cuts:** headline → pill → prompt bar (v0); icon orbit → logo (Superhuman); mascot paper plane wipes each card (Notion Mail); cursor click on a logo flips to a full-bleed colour (Notion AI); push into the prompt box until the typed text fills the frame (Notion 3.0).
