# L3-startups: startup launch videos (YC launches, Product Hunt, seed/Series A/B announcements)

25 videos measured with tools/measure.py. Cuts were re-measured at scdet threshold 5 where cuts/min was under 6 (03, 05, 08, 09, 10, 13). All per-video folders hold metrics.json and the contact sheets. The overview and transitions sheets were viewed for every video; the open sheet for 01, 03 and 13.
